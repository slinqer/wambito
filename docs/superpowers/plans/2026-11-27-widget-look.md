# Widget look (PR 3 of 3) Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** The Android widget draws its figure in the app's figure typeface, its ＋ and 🎤 like the app's bottom bar, and the widget picker shows a real preview of each size.

**Architecture:** Kotlin only (`apps/mobile/android/.../widget/`). The figure is a bitmap drawn with the font file the Flutter app already ships (`flutter_assets/assets/fonts/…`, no copy in `res/font`), white and tinted by Glance (`ColorFilter.tint`), so the day, night and phone themes need no second bitmap. Its width is capped by the room the layout leaves (`figureRoom`), shrinking the type before cutting it. The buttons are two stacked rounded shapes (the ledge in `brandEdge` under the face in `brand`) from a shape drawable, since Glance has no shadows and `cornerRadius` does nothing before Android 12. The previews are rendered by a Robolectric test (native graphics) through `GlanceRemoteViews`, copied into `res/drawable-nodpi` by a script.

**Tech Stack:** Kotlin, Jetpack Glance 1.2, Robolectric 4.16 (native graphics).

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-26-hide-amounts-design.md`, "Widget figure in Nunito", "Widget buttons like the app's", "Picker preview". Stacked on PR 2 (`feat/swipe-haptics`).

## Global Constraints

- Ruling: the figure uses Fredoka 700 with tabular figures, home's hero (`LkText.amountXl`), not Nunito: the spec says "as on home's hero" and the hero is Fredoka; Nunito is the app's text face. One constant to change.
- The figure's description is the amount, or "Monto oculto" when hidden (`amountDescription`), plus the stale text where the layout has no stale line.
- Labels (month, "Te quedan", stale) keep the system font.
- ＋ and 🎤: brand face, `brandEdge` ledge under it, white icons from `icons.json` (`plus`, `mic`), ＋ larger than 🎤.
- Previews: night theme, "Octubre", "Te quedan 312,40 €", the eye open; regenerated by `scripts/gen-widget-previews.sh`, never hand-drawn.

## Review Focus

- A long amount (`$1.234.567.890`) in the small 2×1: shrinks, then cuts, never pushes the eye or the buttons out.
- The font file missing (unit tests, a future asset move): falls back to the bold system face, no crash.
- The phone theme ("system"): the tinted figure follows day and night like the labels.
- Android 8-11: the buttons keep their rounded corners (shape drawable, not `cornerRadius`).
- TalkBack: the figure is read once, as before.
