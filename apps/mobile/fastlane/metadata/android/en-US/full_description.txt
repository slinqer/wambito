Wambito is your budget, and you'll actually enjoy looking after it: log an expense in seconds and see where every buck goes.

<b>What you can do</b>
<ul>
<li>Log an expense with the number pad: amount, category, done.</li>
<li>Say it or type it as one sentence, like "spent 12 on lunch and 3 on coffee". Wambito splits it into entries and you review them before saving; you can remove them or undo.</li>
<li>See what you have spent this month and your entries, grouped by day.</li>
<li>A home-screen widget with ＋ and 🎤, and a Quick Settings tile, to log without hunting for the app.</li>
<li>In Spanish, English, Italian and Portuguese, night or day, with soft sounds and vibration you can turn off.</li>
</ul>

<b>No email, no passwords</b>
Try it without an email, as a guest, and add your email later without losing what you logged. With an email you sign in with a 6-digit code or a link, and if you like with a passkey (your fingerprint or phone lock; it needs Android 14 or newer). Without an email, your data stays encrypted on the server and only this phone opens it: make a recovery key or add a passkey (Wambito reminds you on the home screen) to get it back if you lose your phone or delete the app; without either, it is lost. An account with no email is deleted after 12 months without opening Wambito, or 24 if it has a recovery key or a passkey.

<b>Privacy</b>
Your finances are end-to-end encrypted: they are encrypted on your phone before they leave it, and the server keeps only encrypted text it can't read. No ads, no analytics, no automatic crash reports, no Google Play Services or Firebase. Dictation (in Spanish and English, and in beta in Italian and Portuguese) runs on your phone, offline, with the free Vosk voice models (Apache-2.0): what you say never leaves it. The model for your language downloads once (30 to 50 MB) and you can delete it in Settings. The app asks only for the permissions it uses: internet, microphone (for dictation), vibration, notices, and what the widget needs to refresh (knowing whether there is a network, and rescheduling after the phone restarts).

There is just one notice, local and optional: "Due today…" at 9:00 with the day's recurring payments.

<b>It needs a Wambito server</b>
The app keeps your data on a Wambito server. By default it uses the author's (app.wambito.com), which for now is a personal install and may limit who can add an email. You can point it at your own in Settings: the server is free software too.

<b>What stays on the web</b>
Categories, recurring payments and reports are on the web; the app links you there. Soon you'll be able to import and download your data again, now encrypted.
